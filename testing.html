What is this page? / How to read it ▾

What is this?

A live tracker for your kingdom's Monument event. It shows what chapter is happening right now, what's coming up next, and what's already done — so you always know what to do and how much time you have.

The three sections

  • Top card: the chapter happening right now, with a countdown to when it ends.
  • Upcoming: what's coming next. Tap a chapter to see its exact times.
  • Completed: chapters already finished. Tap to see the list.

🔑 Bonus windows

Some chapters unlock a bonus a bit after they end (a Pass, Shrine, or Lost Temple). Your alliance needs to be holding it when the timer runs out. You'll see it appear in the top card with its own countdown.

Good to know

  • Times are in UTC — convert to your own time zone.
  • The page updates itself every minute. No need to refresh.
  • When a chapter ends, the next one starts automatically.
